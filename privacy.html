Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Tiny Wonder Trails (“we”, “us”) handles information on this website and in Tiny Wonder Studio, the internal tool we use to publish our episodes to our YouTube channel. Tiny Wonder Studio is used only by the owner of the Tiny Wonder Trails channel. It is not offered to the public.

1. We use YouTube API Services

Tiny Wonder Studio uses YouTube API Services to publish videos to our YouTube channel. By using Tiny Wonder Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Before connecting a YouTube account, the user of Tiny Wonder Studio must read and accept this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service; the tool asks for this acceptance when the account is connected.

2. What Tiny Wonder Studio accesses

The channel owner signs in with their own Google Account through Google's secure sign-in (OAuth 2.0). We never see or store the account password. The owner grants two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to upload episodes we created.
  • Manage the channel's YouTube content (youtube) — to set a custom thumbnail on our uploads and to check our own videos' upload and processing status.

With these permissions Tiny Wonder Studio only:

  • uploads videos that we created ourselves, with their title, description, tags, thumbnail, scheduled publish time and the “made for kids” setting;
  • reads back information about our own uploads (whether an upload finished and whether the video still exists) and, when the account is first connected, our channel's name and ID to confirm that the right channel is connected.

Tiny Wonder Studio does not access other people's YouTube accounts, viewers' personal information, comments, subscriber lists, messages, watch history or analytics, and it never downloads videos from YouTube.

3. What we store, and for how long

  • Sign-in token. The access token Google issues after sign-in is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file that only that computer account can read. It is used only to publish our videos and is kept until access is revoked (see section 6).
  • IDs of our uploaded videos. After an upload we keep the video's YouTube ID in a local database on the same computer, so we know which episode was published. Tiny Wonder Studio re-confirms these IDs with YouTube at least every 7 days. If a video no longer exists, its ID is deleted immediately, and any ID that cannot be re-confirmed within 30 days is deleted.
  • We do not store any other data obtained from YouTube API Services.

The episode files we upload (video, audio, thumbnail, script) are our own creative work and are kept on our computer as part of making the series. They are not obtained from YouTube.

4. How we use and share information

We use the information above only to publish and keep track of our own episodes. We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through YouTube API Services, and we do not use it for advertising.

We use the following services to make the series. No data obtained from YouTube API Services is sent to any of them.

  • Google Gemini — helps us write drafts of episode scripts from our own story ideas.
  • Telegram — sends the channel owner each new script and a preview of each finished video so that a person can approve it before publishing.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. Cookies and device information

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it does not load scripts or fonts from other websites. Tiny Wonder Studio runs only on the channel owner's computer and does not place cookies on, or collect information from, anyone else's device.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access to a YouTube account can be revoked at any time in either of these ways:

  • In Google's security settings at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions — remove “Tiny Wonder”. Tiny Wonder Studio then can no longer re-confirm stored video IDs, and they are deleted within 30 days.
  • In Tiny Wonder Studio itself with its youtube-revoke command, which immediately revokes the token with Google and deletes the token and all stored YouTube data.

To ask for any data to be deleted sooner, contact us (section 9).

7. Children's privacy

Our videos are made for children aged 2–5 and are marked “made for kids” on YouTube. We do not collect personal information from children, or from anyone, through this website or our videos. When children watch our videos on YouTube, YouTube's own policies apply, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Security

The sign-in token and the stored video IDs never leave the channel owner's computer except when Tiny Wonder Studio sends requests to YouTube. The token file can be read only by the owner's computer account.

9. Contact

A contact address for questions or complaints about our privacy practices will be published here shortly.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date. If Tiny Wonder Studio's use of YouTube data changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
